About the ministry

Truth, compassion, and service rooted in Christ.

Apostle Cornelius Hamilton is originally from Miami, Florida but has resided in Tampa since his adolescence. After years of ministry and community service, he founded True Prosperity Evangelist Outreach in 2013 with a heart for the lost and a passion for the Kingdom of God.

The ministry operates a full-time feeding mission, clothing closet, food pantry, employment preparation services, and weekly outreach programs to meet practical and spiritual needs in the community.
