Who We Are

A Tampa ministry built on faith, service, and a heart for people.

Apostle Cornelius Hamilton is originally from Miami, Florida but has resided in Tampa, Florida since an adolescent. A graduate of The River Bible Institute, he has a passion for the lost and makes his mission to increase God’s Kingdom by living a life focused on soul winning.

Although he has been in ministry for over a decade, he founded True Prosperity Evangelist Outreach in 2013. It is a Spirit-led ministry operating a full-time feeding mission, clothing closet, food pantry, employment preparation services, AA/NA/Transitional Housing referrals, spiritual guidance, and weekly outreaches.

With the motto “God Over Everything,” Apostle Hamilton takes the ministry beyond the church and into the community. His work has reached across the southeastern United States, and he continues to expand the ministry online.

The Tampa Bay Times has described him as a “Street Samaritan.” His service has also been featured by local news stations and the Florida Sentinel Bulletin.

Mrs. Devlyn Hamilton

A former employee of 95.7 The Beat, now iHeart Radio, Nia Broadcasting WTMP 1150 AM & 96.7 FM in Tampa and WRXB 1590 AM in St Pete. A reformed gossip blogger, she has devoted her life to increasing the Kingdom of God.

Originally from Fayetteville, North Carolina, she moved to Tampa as a young teenager and made the city her home. She currently partners with her husband in operating True Prosperity Evangelist Outreach.

Mission in Action

  • Food and clothing support for families
  • Employment preparation and referrals
  • Weekly community outreach and soul winning
  • Spiritual guidance, prayer, and counseling
